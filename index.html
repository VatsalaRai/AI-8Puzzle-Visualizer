THE 8-PUZZLE

The 8-Puzzle is a sliding tile puzzle on a 3 by 3 grid. Eight numbered tiles share the board with one empty space called the blank. A move slides one tile that touches the blank into it. The task is to turn a starting arrangement into a target arrangement using as few moves, or as little cost, as possible.

There are 9! = 362,880 possible arrangements, but only half of them, 181,440, can be reached from any one start, because every legal move keeps the parity of the tile inversions unchanged. A blank in a corner has 2 moves, on an edge 3 moves, in the centre 4 moves.

PROBLEM FORMULATION

  • State: the position of the eight tiles and the blank.
  • Initial state: any solvable arrangement you choose or generate.
  • Actions: move the blank up, down, left or right.
  • Goal test: the board equals the goal arrangement.
  • Path cost: 1 per move, or the tile number per move (both are supported).
HOW TO PLAY

Blank Quest turns the puzzle into a game and keeps the search algorithm working behind the scenes.

  • Pick or generate a puzzle on the Setup page.
  • On the Play page, click a tile next to the blank, or use the arrow keys or W A S D to slide the blank.
  • Every move makes a sound. A right move is one that lies on a cheapest path to the goal and gets a bright rising chime. A wrong move leads you away and gets a low buzz with a message saying so.
  • The shortest answer stays hidden until you solve the puzzle, or give up. Then Uniform Cost Search reveals the optimal solution and compares it with yours.
  • Want to see the algorithm think? The Solver page replays the search node by node.

ASSIGNED ALGORITHM

Uniform Cost Search always expands the cheapest unexpanded node first. It is complete and optimal when every step costs more than zero.
